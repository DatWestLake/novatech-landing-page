THU MUC ANH CUA LANDING PAGE
============================

=== QUY TRINH THEM ANH SAN PHAM (4 buoc) ===

BUOC 1 - THU NHO ANH TRUOC KHI DUNG
   Anh chup goc thuong rat nang (5-8MB). Dua thang len web se rat cham.
   Vao trang  https://squoosh.app  (mien phi, khong can cai):
     - Keo tha anh vao
     - Chon dinh dang WebP, chat luong 80
     - Chinh chieu rong (Resize) ve 900px
     - Bam Download
   Muc tieu: moi anh duoi 50KB.

BUOC 2 - DAT TEN DE HIEU, CHEP VAO THU MUC NAY
   Dat ten khong dau, khong khoang trang. Vi du:
     dh80n1t-1.webp   (anh chinh)
     dh80n1t-2.webp   (anh phu)
     dh80n1t-3.webp
   Chep tat ca vao thu muc "images" nay.

BUOC 3 - KHAI BAO TRONG index.html
   Mo index.html, tim muc "4. DANH SACH SAN PHAM", sua san pham tuong ung:

     anh: "images/dh80n1t-1.webp",     <-- anh chinh, hien tren the
     anhThem: [                         <-- cac anh phu (co the bo qua)
       "images/dh80n1t-2.webp",
       "images/dh80n1t-3.webp"
     ],

   LUU Y DAU CAU:
     - Moi duong dan boc trong dau nhay kep "..."
     - Giua cac dong trong ngoac vuong co dau phay ,
     - DONG CUOI CUNG trong ngoac vuong KHONG co dau phay
     - Sau dau ngoac vuong dong ] phai co dau phay ,

BUOC 4 - LUU FILE, MO LAI TRANG
   Neu co tu 2 anh tro len, trang tu hien day o vuong nho ben duoi anh chinh.
   Bam vao o nao thi anh chinh doi sang anh do.

=== POPUP XEM ANH LON ===
   Bam vao anh chinh tren the san pham -> anh mo to ra giua man hinh.
   Trong popup:
     - Mui ten trai/phai hoac phim <- -> : chuyen anh
     - Bam nen toi, nut X, hoac phim Esc : dong lai
     - Goc duoi hien so thu tu, vi du "3 / 5"
   Popup dung chung cho moi san pham, khong phai khai bao gi them.
   Anh nen rong khoang 1200px de popup hien ro net.

=== CAC TRUONG HOP TU DONG XU LY ===
   - Chi co 1 anh            -> khong hien day o nho
   - Duong dan anh sai       -> tu quay ve hinh ve minh hoa, trang khong vo
   - Mot anh phu bi thieu    -> o vuong cua anh do tu bien mat
   - Khong khai bao anh nao  -> dung hinh ve minh hoa san co
